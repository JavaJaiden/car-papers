Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Car Papers is made by narb LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Car Papers has no accounts and no servers. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your iPhone

The photos you add, the details you confirm and your reminder settings are saved on your iPhone, inside the app. If you back up your iPhone, they are part of that backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Reading your documents

Reading text from your photos and answering your questions happen on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan a document. The app sees only the photos you choose.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

Links

When you tap Renew now, your state's website opens in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

Deleting your data

Use Delete everything in Settings, or delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
