How do I add a document?

Tap Add on the home screen, choose the kind of document, then scan the paper or choose a photo. Check the expiry date the app read, and tap save.

The app read the wrong date. What do I do?

On "Check the details", tap the date and pick the right one before you save. To fix a saved document, open it, tap Edit and change the expiry date.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open the iPhone's Settings app, tap Notifications, tap Car Papers and turn on Allow Notifications. Then open the document in Car Papers and check that its reminder switches are on.

How do I show my documents quickly?

Tap Show My Documents on the home screen. For one-tap access, add a Car Papers widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, or add the Car Papers button to Control Center.

Will my documents move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one. Your documents are part of your iPhone's backup. Car Papers has no account, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Is the license photo a legal ID?

No. It is a backup copy for your records. Keep carrying your real license. Rules for showing registration and insurance on a phone differ from state to state, so check what your state accepts.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings in Car Papers and tap Delete everything, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Is Car Papers connected to the DMV or my insurer?

No. Car Papers is not a government app and is not connected to any insurer. It keeps copies of your own documents on your iPhone.
