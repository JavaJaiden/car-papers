Add a paper once.
Car Papers keeps it ready.

  1. Step 1

    Take a photo.

    Scan the paper or pick a screenshot. Car Papers reads the expiry date, and on iPhones with Apple Intelligence it fills in your insurer too. You check everything before it's saved.

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Open the app and add a photo.

How do I get started?

Download it and open it. There is no account to make. Name your car, add a photo of your registration or insurance card, and choose whether you want reminders. You can skip any of it and come back later.

Can you see my documents?

No. Your photos are saved on your iPhone, inside Car Papers. We have no accounts and no servers, so there is nothing for us to see.

What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Your papers are part of your own iPhone backup. Set up your new iPhone from that backup and they come with you.

Is the photo of my license a legal ID?

No. It is a backup copy for your records, not a legal ID. Keep carrying your real license.

Will my state accept papers on my phone?

Rules for showing registration and insurance on a phone differ from state to state. Car Papers is not a government app, so check what your state accepts.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later, including iPhone Duo. Reading the expiry date works on all of them. Filling in your insurer and policy number, and asking questions, need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Is there an Android version?

It's in development. For now, Car Papers is for iPhone. The Android version will also need no sign-in, and nothing will leave your phone.
