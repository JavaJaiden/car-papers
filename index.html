Your car papers, ready when you need them.

Keep your registration and insurance card on your iPhone. Show them in one tap, and get a reminder before either one expires.

What it does

Three things, done simply.

No menus to learn. One big button shows your documents. The app does the remembering.

01

Take a photo and you're done

Scan the paper or pick a screenshot. Car Papers reads the expiry date for you, and you check it before anything is saved.

02

Show them in one tap

One button opens your documents full screen with the brightness turned up. Add a widget and they are one tap from your Lock Screen.

03

Never miss a renewal

A reminder 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day. Nothing else. One tap opens your state's motor vehicle website.

Also included

Made for the day you actually need it.

  • Widgets and a Control Center buttonOpen your papers from the Home Screen, the Lock Screen or Control Center.
  • More than one carEach car keeps its own registration and insurance card.
  • A backup copy of your driver's licenseFor your records if you lose your wallet. It does not replace the card.
  • Ask your papersOn iPhones with Apple Intelligence, type a question such as "What's my policy number?"
Private by design

Nothing leaves your iPhone.

We built Car Papers so we never see your documents. There is nothing for us to lose, sell or leak.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and add a photo.

No servers

Your photos are saved on your iPhone and nowhere else. The app has no networking code.

Read on your phone

Reading the date and answering questions happen on your iPhone, not in the cloud.

Good to know

Car Papers is not a government app.

A photo of your driver's license is a backup copy for your records, not a legal ID, so keep carrying your real license. Rules for showing registration and insurance on a phone differ from state to state. Check what your state accepts.
